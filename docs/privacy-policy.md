## 4. Categories of personal data, purposes and legal bases

### 4.1. Access to the Website

4.1.1. When the Website is accessed, our hosting and content delivery
providers process technical data relating to each request, namely: the IP address from
which the request originates, the resource requested, the date and time of the
request, the HTTP response status, the user agent string (browser and operating
system version) and, where transmitted by the browser, the referring address.

4.1.2. The pages and static assets of the Website are delivered through the
network of Cloudflare, Inc. ("Cloudflare"), which also stands in front of the
server referred to in the following sentence. The content management system
from which the Website is produced, the media files uploaded to it and the
submissions referred to in clause 4.5 are held on a virtual machine which we
operate ourselves on Microsoft Azure infrastructure, in the West Europe region
(Netherlands).

4.1.3. **Purpose.** Delivery of the Website, maintenance of its availability and
integrity, prevention and investigation of abuse, and protection against
attacks directed at the Website.

4.1.4. **Legal basis.** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in
operating a secure and functioning website.

### 4.2. Audience measurement

4.2.1. We measure the use of the Website by means of Umami, an analytics
application which we operate ourselves on the virtual machine referred to in
clause 4.1.2. The data which it records are held on that machine and are not
transmitted to the publisher of that application or to any other recipient.

4.2.2. Umami operates without cookies and without any other form of storage on
or access to the terminal equipment of the visitor. It does not retain IP
addresses and does not permit the tracking of visitors across websites or the
construction of individual profiles. The following data are recorded in respect
of each page view: the page viewed; the referring address; the browser,
operating system, device type and screen dimensions; and the country of origin
of the request, derived from the IP address, which is not itself retained.

4.2.3. For the purpose of distinguishing repeat page views within a single day,
Umami generates a one-way hash from the IP address, the user agent and a secret
value which is rotated every twenty-four hours. By reason of such rotation, the
resulting identifier cannot be attributed to a data subject and ceases to
correspond to the same visitor after that period.

4.2.4. **Purpose.** Measurement of audience and of the use made of individual
pages, for the purpose of maintaining and improving the content and structure
of the Website.

4.2.5. **Legal basis.** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in
understanding how the Website is used. As no information is stored on, or
accessed from, the terminal equipment of the visitor, the consent requirement
under Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC, as transposed by Law no. 506/2004,
does not apply to this processing.

### 4.3. Map functionality

4.3.1. Certain pages of the Website display a map. The map is rendered in the
visitor's browser by means of the MapLibre GL JS library, which is served from
our own domain. The map imagery is requested by the visitor's browser directly
from Stadia Maps ("**Stadia Maps**") as the map is displayed.

4.3.2. By reason of that direct request, Stadia Maps receives the visitor's IP
address, the user agent string and the identifiers of the map imagery
requested, which disclose the geographic area displayed. No further data are
transmitted to Stadia Maps by us, and we receive no data concerning the visitor
in return. Stadia Maps acts as our processor under a data processing agreement
concluded pursuant to Article 28 GDPR. Its privacy notice is available at
https://stadiamaps.com/privacy-policy/.

4.3.3. The map is loaded upon the opening of a page containing it, and not upon
interaction with it.

4.3.4. Where a map is accompanied by a link permitting the location to be opened
in an external mapping service, no data are transmitted to the operator of that
service unless and until the link is activated by the visitor, at which point
the visitor leaves the Website and the privacy notice of that operator applies.

4.3.5. **Purpose.** Display of the geographic location of our premises.

4.3.6. **Legal basis.** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in
indicating our location to visitors.

### 4.4. Embedded video content

4.4.1. Certain pages of the Website incorporate video content hosted on
YouTube, a service provided in the European Economic Area by Google Ireland
Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("**Google**").

4.4.2. The loading of a YouTube player constitutes the incorporation of third
party content into the Website. Upon such loading, Google receives the
visitor's IP address, the address of the page on which the player appears and
information concerning the visitor's browser, and may store information on, and
access information stored on, the visitor's terminal equipment, including
cookies and comparable identifiers. Where the visitor is at that time
authenticated to a Google account, Google is able to attribute such activity to
that account. Such processing may occur irrespective of whether playback is
initiated. The subsequent processing of those data is carried out by Google for
its own purposes and under its own privacy policy, available at
https://policies.google.com/privacy, and is outside our control.

4.4.3. For that reason, no YouTube player is loaded, and no data are
transmitted to Google, unless the visitor first requests playback. Until such
request, the location of the video displays a static image together with a
notice to that effect. Activation of the player by the visitor constitutes the
consent referred to in clause 4.4.4.

4.4.4. **Legal basis.** Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, the consent of the data subject,
and Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC as transposed by Law no. 506/2004 in
respect of the storage of and access to information on terminal equipment.
Consent may be withdrawn at any time, with effect for the future, by reloading
the page without requesting playback; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness
of processing carried out prior to it.

### 4.5. Forms submitted through the Website

4.5.1. Where a contact, enquiry, application or comparable form is submitted
through the Website, we process the data entered into the fields of that form,
which comprise, depending on the form concerned, the name, electronic mail
address, telephone number and message of the sender, together with any further
data which the relevant form requests. Submissions are stored in the content
management system referred to in clause 4.1.2.

4.5.2. **Purpose.** Receipt of, and response to, the communication, and the
conduct of any subsequent correspondence or pre-contractual exchange arising
from it.

4.5.3. **Legal basis.** Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where the submission is made with
a view to taking steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering
into a contract or to the performance of a contract; and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR,
our legitimate interest in responding to communications addressed to us.

4.5.4. The provision of the data marked as mandatory in a form is necessary in
order for us to respond. The provision of any further data is optional and a
failure to provide it carries no consequence other than the inability to take
it into account.

4.5.5. Data subjects are requested not to submit, by means of a web form, data
falling within the special categories referred to in Article 9(1) GDPR. Where
communication of such data is necessary, an appropriate means of transmission
may be requested from us at the address indicated in clause 1.3.

4.5.6. Where we notify ourselves of a submission, or correspond with the sender
in reply to it, by electronic mail, that correspondence is carried by Google
Workspace, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited.

### 4.6. Display preferences

4.6.1. Where the visitor selects a light or dark appearance for the Website,
that selection is stored in the local storage of the visitor's browser under
the key `payload-theme`. The stored value is not transmitted to us or to any
third party and is not accessible to us.

4.6.2. **Legal basis.** Article 5(3), second sentence, of Directive
2002/58/EC, as transposed by Law no. 506/2004, the storage being strictly
necessary in order to provide a functionality expressly requested by the
visitor. The stored value is removed upon clearing the browsing data of the
browser concerned.

## 9. Rights of the data subject

9.1. Subject to the conditions laid down in the GDPR, data subjects have the
right:

a) to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning them are being
processed and, where that is the case, access to those data and to the
information referred to in Article 15 GDPR;

b) to obtain the rectification of inaccurate personal data and the completion of
incomplete personal data (Article 16 GDPR);

c) to obtain the erasure of personal data concerning them where one of the
grounds in Article 17 GDPR applies;

d) to obtain the restriction of processing in the circumstances set out in
Article 18 GDPR;

e) to receive the personal data which they have provided to us in a structured,
commonly used and machine-readable format and to transmit those data to another
controller, where the processing is based on consent or on a contract and is
carried out by automated means (Article 20 GDPR);

f) to object, on grounds relating to their particular situation, at any time to
processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, including the processing described in
clauses 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 and 4.5, in which case we shall cease such processing
unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds which override the
interests, rights and freedoms of the data subject, or the processing is
required for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (Article 21
GDPR);

g) to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on Article 6(1)(a)
GDPR, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before
its withdrawal.

9.2. The rights set out in clause 9.1 may be exercised by request addressed as
provided in clause 1.3. We shall provide information on action taken without
undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt of the request, which
period may be extended by two further months where necessary, taking into
account the complexity and number of requests, in which case the data subject
shall be informed of the extension and of the reasons for the delay within one
month of receipt. No fee is charged, save in the circumstances provided for in
Article 12(5) GDPR.

9.3. Where necessary in order to confirm the identity of the requesting person,
we may request the provision of additional information for that purpose, in
accordance with Article 12(6) GDPR.

9.4. Data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory
authority, in particular in the Member State of their habitual residence, place
of work or place of the alleged infringement. The supervisory authority
competent in Romania is the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării
Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru nos.
28-30, Sector 1, 010336 Bucharest, electronic mail
anspdcp@dataprotection.ro, www.dataprotection.ro. The exercise of that right is
without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedy.
